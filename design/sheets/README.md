# Sage's sheets

The sheets Sage's look was decided on, in the order they were made (all on 2026-11-16), kept as the reference for every look that comes after. Each was rendered from the art as it stood that day, at the board's 240 by 240, on the prism's black. They are part of Sage's art: see [LICENSE-ART.md](../../LICENSE-ART.md).

A new look, or a look changed at scale, gets its sheet here before it is baked (CLAUDE.md), numbered after the last one, with a line below saying what it shows and what was decided.

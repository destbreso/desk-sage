Sources, and what is not published

Everything here is drawn from numbers, at its real size in millimetres; none of Waveshare's pictures is used. The sizes come from Waveshare's dimension drawings, the parts from its schematic, and their places on the component side are measured on its numbered photo, at the scale of the M2 holes (22.55 mm apart). A package's size is its datasheet's where the package is known, otherwise it is measured on the photo.

    Also from this repository: the firmware (firmware/platformio.ini, firmware/src/main.cpp) for the SPI clock, the motion sensor's I2C pins and the mirrored drawing, and the printed monitor mount (enclosure/lump_monitor_mount.scad) for the stand measured by hand and its USB at the back.

    Not published, and marked where it is drawn: where the CH343P, the QMI8658A and the WS2812B sit on the display side (shown together, dashed, as positions not published); the USB-C's place along its edge (inferred from four plated slots of its shell on the component side); the inside of the stand and of the case, and whether the -C's stand holds the board in the -B's case or bare (it is drawn inside the case); what the metal back between the display and the board is; how the standoffs are held in the board; the thicknesses of the display's layers and the driver's size; the cube's coating; the antenna's part number; the crystal's package (measured at about 2.5 by 2.0 mm); and the dozens of small resistors and capacitors, of which only the clearly visible 0603 parts are drawn.

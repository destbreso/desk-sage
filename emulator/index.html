Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom. A click is a tap.

The board ESP32-S3, 240 x 240

It plays the .bin as the firmware does and talks to the bridge in the board's own line protocol.

    Serial

      Every command the board understands, as the bridge sends it over the serial line. An example goes into the box above; Enter sends it. While you type, the box suggests the commands, the moods and the acts of the art that is loaded (arrows to move, Tab or Enter to take one, Escape to close). Any line but w and W also counts as hearing from the bridge.
